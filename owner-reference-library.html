Not sure what hot tub you have?

If it's a Cal Spas: look for the identification or serial-number plate on the outside of the spa. Copy the model and serial details exactly; if the manufacturing year isn't clear, ask HTFO to help interpret it. Do not enter your serial number publicly.

If it's another brand: check the owner's paperwork, the cabinet label (if present) or the control panel for a brand or model identifier. We do not assume all brands use the same plate location.

What does your control panel look like?

Pick the closest type. These are identification hints, not confirmed model matches.

If you cannot identify the panel, our team can help with a photograph and any model details you can locate.
