## Production standard

1. **Identification and safety (0:00–0:40):** show real photo and on-screen keypad part number; explain this tutorial is for that keypad and that variations exist.
2. **Daily basics (0:40–2:00):** actual screen capture or camera demonstration of temperature set point, pumps/jets, lights; indicate when features are not equipped.
3. **Heat behavior (2:00–3:15):** actual control's heat or economy/rest settings (names differ by model); show how to return to normal heating and distinguish displayed temp from target.
4. **Filtration (3:15–4:35):** review active schedule; when/how owner can adjust; explain not to reduce filtration without understanding water needs.
5. **Optional settings (4:35–5:40):** time, lock, Wi-Fi/audio *only when that keypad supports it and equipment is installed*.
6. **Messages and help (5:40–7:00):** identify where errors appear, not fabricate codes; avoid unsafe electrical troubleshooting; refer to official troubleshooting and 412-326-0361 for service.
7. **Ending (7:00–7:15):** Bubbles + real HTFO logo and link to the model-year archive. No expensive animation until approved.

Use original panel photography/video or expressly licensed manufacturer assets, not generated fake control panels. Shoot clean, direct 16:9 master; subtitles; deliver one chapter-marked video per keypad. Keep chapter source clips privately to update a future firmware revision without multiplying public videos.

## Script A — Balboa SpaTouch 2 (2026 Cal Escape Plus baseline)

**Opening narration (chapter 1):**
"Welcome to your Hot Tub Factory Outlet control panel tutorial. This guide is for the Balboa SpaTouch 2 touchscreen. Compare the screen in this video to your own panel before making changes. Different factory options and replacement controls can look different."

**Chapter 2 narration — daily basics:**
"Start on the main screen. Check the water temperature and the temperature you have set. Use the on-screen temperature control to make an adjustment, then confirm the new set temperature. Next we'll show the pump or jets controls and the light control, using a real SpaTouch 2 so you can follow every tap. Your spa may have fewer pumps or different lighting options than the one shown."
**SHOT REQUIRED:** physical SpaTouch 2 showing actual tap paths, active pump(s), and illumination. DO NOT fill in guessed button names or target values.

**Chapter 3 narration — heating:**
"The heating settings determine how your spa maintains its temperature. Before selecting an energy-saving mode, understand how that mode changes when the water heats. We'll show the heating screen and how to return to your preferred operating mode."
**SHOT REQUIRED:** actual SpaTouch 2 heat settings, confirmed UI labels and exact screen sequence from official guide.

**Chapter 4 narration — filtration:**
"Your spa's filtration schedule helps circulate and clean the water. We'll open the filter-cycle settings, review the existing schedule, and demonstrate how to make and save a change if your setup permits it. Don't assume that a shorter cycle is appropriate for every spa."
**Source:** https://www.balboawater.com/user-guides/spa-touch-2/set-filter-cycle-time/

**Chapter 5 narration — preferences:**
"We'll cover the time display, screen lock and other preferences that your installed system supports. We won't demonstrate Wi-Fi, music or additional equipment unless it is actually fitted to the spa in this video."
**Sources:** https://www.balboawater.com/user-guides/spa-touch-2/set-time-of-day/ and https://www.balboawater.com/user-guides/spa-touch-2/lock-the-panel-and-or-settings/

**Chapter 6 narration — troubleshooting:**
"If the panel shows an alert, read the message before adjusting settings. Some messages require service. We will show how to find the information on your screen and where to locate the exact fault-code explanation. Never open energized equipment or bypass safety devices."
**SHOT REQUIRED:** real display demo; no fabricated fault conditions.
**Closing:** "Keep this video bookmarked. If you need help, call Hot Tub Factory Outlet at 412-326-0361."

**Production blockers:** Real SpaTouch 2 keypad footage (or authorized accurate screen capture) and validation that the demonstrated menu labels match the deployed version. Script is a narration draft; final film requires filming the actual taps.
