What should you compare when buying a hot tub in Pittsburgh?

Start with fit and use: how many people will use it, whether you want lounge or open seating, the type of hydrotherapy you want, the available space and your budget. Then compare insulation, controls, warranty coverage, delivery support and who will service the spa after the sale. Pittsburgh winters make insulation and ongoing service especially worth understanding before you buy.
