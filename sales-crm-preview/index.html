Automatic Email Nurture

Email is the primary automated nurture channel. It stays broad and follows the product category captured from the ad, form, or landing page. Default cadence: 10 emails total on Days 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, and 15. Brand/model messaging is reserved for targeted campaigns after staff learns what the shopper actually likes.

Separate SMS Nurture

Texting is intentionally lighter than email. Default plan: 4 SMS touches, only for contacts with SMS consent. Suggested cadence: Days 1, 5, 10, and 15. If the customer replies, the automated SMS sequence pauses so a salesperson can take over.

Preferred channel: the lead record can be marked Email, SMS, Phone, or No preference. Staff can change it as they learn how the customer actually responds.

Default New-Lead Workflow

TRIGGER

New lead enters CRM

1. Create/open opportunity
2. Assign salesperson
3. Start the 10-touch product-category email nurture
4. If SMS consent exists, start the separate lighter SMS sequence
5. Create salesperson follow-up tasks alongside nurture
6. Log every automated email/text to the shared customer timeline
7. Pause the relevant automation when the customer replies
8. Stop nurture immediately when Sold, Lost, or opted out

Automation Guardrails

• Email opt-out stops automated and bulk email
• SMS opt-out stops automated and bulk text
• Customer reply pauses that channel's automation
• Sold/Lost stops pre-sale nurture
• Staff can manually pause/resume nurture
• Every touch remains visible to the whole team in the shared timeline
• Brand/model campaigns are salesperson-driven, not part of the generic new-lead sequence
Example: a Meta hot-tub lead receives the Hot Tub email nurture. A swim-spa ad lead receives the Swim Spa email nurture. Once a salesperson records “Vita / Rendezvous,” that person becomes eligible for Vita/Rendezvous targeted campaigns.
