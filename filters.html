Narrow by cartridge measurements

Measure outside diameter and end-cap-to-end-cap length to about 1/16 inch. Do not include handles or extensions. Also measure the inside diameter of the top and bottom openings when applicable.

Don’t force a match. Two filters can share the same outside diameter and length but use different openings, handles or threads. If more than one possibility remains, compare the top and bottom carefully or bring the old filter to either HTFO showroom.
