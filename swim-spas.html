Start with your goal

What do you want your water to do?

Don't start with model numbers. Start with the reason you're considering a swim spa, then narrow the size, current system and seating from there.

01

Swim & Train

Prioritize a clear swim lane, stronger current and room for aquatic exercise. Best for swimmers, fitness-focused buyers and consistent training.

02

Therapy & Recovery

Look for massage seating plus room for low-impact movement, stretching and recovery in warm water.

03

Family & Fun

Choose more seating and usable open-water space when kids, friends and entertaining matter as much as exercise.

Bubbles' Tip

A bigger swim spa isn't automatically a better swim spa. Tell us whether you're really buying it to swim, exercise, recover, entertain — or do all four — and we'll help narrow the field.

Plan before delivery day

The swim spa is only half the project.

Site preparation matters. We'll help you think through the installation before you commit to a model.

Foundation

A filled swim spa is extremely heavy and needs a properly designed, flat, stable, load-bearing foundation. The exact requirement should follow the selected model's installation specifications.

Electrical

Swim spas generally require dedicated hardwired electrical service. A qualified electrician should size and install the circuit according to the exact model and applicable electrical codes.

Delivery Access

These are big units. Access, equipment clearance and placement need to be planned in advance; some installations may require crane or professional rigging.

Installation requirements vary by model and site. Always use the current manufacturer's manual and qualified local professionals for structural, electrical, permitting and installation decisions.
