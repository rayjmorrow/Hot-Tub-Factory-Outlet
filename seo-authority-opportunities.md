# HTFO / Pittsburgh Pool Company authority opportunities

Updated: 2026-09-26

## Priority 1 — strongest local/industry citations

1. **Monroeville Area Chamber of Commerce** — Public member directory plus member spotlight opportunities. Strong fit for the Monroeville showroom. Verify/join listing and link to the Monroeville/Pittsburgh hot-tub pages.
2. **Pittsburgh North Regional Chamber** — Searchable member directory serving Allegheny, Beaver and Butler counties from Wexford. Strong fit for Wexford, Cranberry, Mars, Gibsonia and surrounding pages.
3. **Westmoreland County Chamber of Commerce** — Large active member directory. Their Murrysville Business & Professional Association chapter is especially relevant to Murrysville/Delmont/Export/Greensburg reach.
4. **Pool & Hot Tub Alliance (PHTA) Find a Pool Pro** — Industry directory with retailer, hot-tub, service, builder and installer categories. Check membership/listing eligibility for HTFO and Pittsburgh Pool Company.
5. **Cranberry Township official business directory** — Municipality-run searchable directory. Determine listing eligibility for the Wexford location / business service area.

## Priority 3 — local civic/business citations

- Municipality of Murrysville resource/business directory
- Monroeville municipal/resource directory where business submissions are supported
- Visit Monroeville / local visitor guides when business inclusion is available
- Butler County / Cranberry business resources
- Relevant home-and-garden / outdoor-living associations with real editorial standards

## What to avoid

Do not buy bulk links, use fake local directories, publish spun guest posts, or create artificial review/citation networks. Prioritize directories tied to municipalities, chambers, manufacturers, recognized trade groups and real local organizations.

## Outreach angle

Lead with a useful local resource rather than asking only for a backlink: showroom tours, hot-tub winter-energy education, pool planning seminars, grill demonstrations, homeowner checklists, or community-event support. Real participation creates stronger citations and mentions than a paid generic directory link.
