Replacement Covers

Let’s get the fit right the first time.

This guided request walks you through the spa information, shape, measurements and options we need to start a replacement-cover quote. We’ll review the details with you before anything is ordered.

Tell us about your spa.

If you know the manufacturer and model, start there. If not, that’s okay — good measurements and photos can usually get us moving.

Have the old cover? Keep it nearby while measuring. In many cases it gives us the clearest reference for overall size, corner radius, skirt length and hinge direction.

Choose the shape and measure.

Measure across the widest outside edges of the spa. Enter measurements in inches. We’ll verify unusual shapes before ordering.

Core measurements

Tip: When in doubt, slightly larger is safer than too small. We’ll review the final measurements with you.

Cover details.

These details affect how the cover sits, folds and works with your spa. If you’re unsure, choose “Not sure” and we’ll help.

Photos help.

For now, this prototype does not upload files directly. After you send the request, you can reply to the email with a photo of the spa from above, the old cover, and the manufacturer label if available.

Review and send.

Add your contact information, review the summary, then email the request to HTFO. We’ll verify the details before any cover is ordered.

Your cover request

Your email app should open with the cover request already filled in. Attach any helpful photos before sending.
