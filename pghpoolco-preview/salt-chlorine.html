Salt Chlorine Generators: Why or Why Not?

A salt pool is still a chlorine pool. The difference is how the chlorine is produced.

How it works

A salt chlorine generator uses dissolved salt and an electrolytic cell to produce chlorine while the circulation system runs.

Why homeowners like them

  • Steadier sanitizer production
  • Less day-to-day handling of chlorine products
  • Adjustable output
  • Many swimmers prefer the feel of properly balanced salt water

Why they are not automatic perfection

  • Water chemistry still needs attention
  • The salt cell eventually needs replacement
  • Salt concentration and material compatibility matter
  • Cold-water operation can be limited depending on the system
