Gas Heater vs. Electric Heat Pump

The right choice depends on how fast you want heat, how long you maintain temperature and how you use the pool.

Gas Heater

  • Fast temperature rise
  • Good for heating on demand
  • Works well even when outdoor air is cooler
  • Fuel cost and availability matter

Electric Heat Pump

  • Moves heat from surrounding air into the pool
  • Generally slower than gas
  • Can be efficient when conditions are favorable
  • Often attractive for maintaining temperature over longer periods

For Pittsburgh, the key question is how you plan to use the pool. Weekend heat-up and season-long temperature maintenance are different jobs.
