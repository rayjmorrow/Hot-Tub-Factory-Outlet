Choosing Your Pool

Vinyl vs. Fiberglass Pools

Both can be excellent pools. The right answer depends on the property, design goals and the ownership experience you prefer.

Vinyl Liner

  • More freedom in dimensions and custom shapes.
  • Comfortable liner surface underfoot.
  • Useful when the backyard calls for a shape not limited to a factory mold.
  • The liner is a wear item and will eventually need replacement.
  • New liner choices can refresh the look later.

Fiberglass

  • Factory-built shell with a smooth surface.
  • Installation can move quickly after site preparation.
  • No liner replacement.
  • Shapes and dimensions are limited to available shells and transportation constraints.
  • Often attractive to homeowners who want a more standardized installation path.
Our recommendation: do not choose from one sales talking point. Choose based on your yard, the exact shape you want, access, slope, budget and how you want to own the pool over time.

Western Pennsylvania changes the conversation.

Hills, access, retaining walls, drainage and usable patio space can matter as much as the pool shell. A site review may make one construction method a much better fit than another.
