Before you choose the finish

Four things to bring to the conversation.

A few measurements and phone photos can turn a long product list into a practical starting point.

1

Your space

Measure the patio or intended kitchen area. Leave room for people to pass, doors to open and the grill lid to lift.

2

Your cooking style

Think about your usual dinner crowd, weekend gatherings and whether you want just a grill or a complete prep and serving area.

3

Your utilities

Tell us whether you have propane or natural gas nearby, and whether the plan includes refrigeration, lighting or a sink.

4

Your project

Bring photos, your target budget and timing. For a grill replacement, include the current model and the opening measurements.

Final placement, ventilation, cutouts, clearances and utility connections must follow the selected equipment’s instructions and local requirements. Confirm specifications before construction.

Outdoor kitchen questions

Let’s make the choices easier.

Can I buy a grill without a complete island?

Yes. We can help you shop grills separately as well as compare complete outdoor kitchen options. If you are replacing a built-in grill, bring the current grill model and exact cutout dimensions.

Do you offer both Cal Flame and Bull BBQ?

Yes. Hot Tub Factory Outlet is a Cal Flame and Bull BBQ dealer serving Greater Pittsburgh through our Monroeville and Wexford locations. Contact us for current product availability and pricing.

How much does an outdoor kitchen cost?

The grill, island size, countertop, finish and selected accessories all affect the price. Utility work and site preparation also depend on the project. Ask us for a quote based on the layout and equipment you want.

Can you help me plan around an existing pool or patio?

Yes. Bring photos and dimensions so we can discuss an equipment and layout starting point. For a pool project, explore our sister site, Pittsburgh Pool Company, too.

Are the models on display at both showrooms?

Displays and inventory can vary. Call 412-326-0361 before visiting so we can confirm where to see the products you are interested in.
